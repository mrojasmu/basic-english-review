Verb "to be" and Present Continuous

I am
yo soy / estoy
You are
tú eres / estás
He is
él es / está
She is
ella es / está
It is
eso es / está
We are
nosotros somos / estamos
They are
ellos son / están
I am studying.
Estoy estudiando.
She is working.
Ella está trabajando.
They are listening.
Ellos están escuchando.
Are you reading?
¿Estás leyendo?
Ejemplos

Oraciones frecuentes

I am a student.
She is happy.
They are in class.
I am studying English.
She is reading a book.
They are speaking English.
