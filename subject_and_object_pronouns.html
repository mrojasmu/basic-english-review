Materia

Subject and Object Pronouns

I → me
yo → me / mí
you → you
tú → te / ti
he → him
él → lo / a él
she → her
ella → la / a ella
it → it
eso → lo
we → us
nosotros → nos
they → them
ellos → los / a ellos
Ejemplos

Oraciones frecuentes

I like English.
She helps me.
I know him.
They call us.
We see them.
